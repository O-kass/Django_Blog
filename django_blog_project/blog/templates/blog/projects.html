GMM_Simulation

This is a pygame project which uses the gravitational formuala GMM/r squared to simulate objects over a mass

  • HOW TO PLAY
    Click once and then move your mouse to see a white line this line is the trajectory the red dot (the one you just made by clicking) follows.
  • PACKAGES USED
    Math, Pygame
  • TIME TAKEN
    Approx one day of work(6 hours)
  • POSSIBLE EXT
    Add GUI to change planets/environment to simulate different gravitational forces
